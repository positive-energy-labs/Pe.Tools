# Concisify

Shorten your response. Use the least possible words to get the point across, precision and totality are not the goals. <500 words first, then let the user tell you what they want to hear next.

Your response must be understandable at a glance. Generally these tips help:
- **Prose** needs to be scannable. Paragraphs/sentences should be semantically front-heavy and short. 
- **Structured writing** needs predictable semantic and visual patterns. Default to structured, especially tables, for everything that it fits.
- **Sections with titles** clearly separate topics, no wall of text.
- **Excluded info**, caveats, etc. localized to a single closing section, preferably a sentence, maybe a paragraph if its dire.
- Unresolved todos briefly acknowledged. A concise response from you should not indicate completion.
- Abbreviated writing is encouraged. Acronyms, "information" → "info", and/or slashes, glyphs, etc. 

> NOTE: Users may invoke this skill on an already `consisify`-ed response. Treat this as deliberate and trigger this skill again.
