# Goal

**Lead a Crusade.** One holy goal, many waves of pilgrims, most of whom do not return. The commander does not fight; the commander reads the field, keeps what held, and sends the next wave. A crusade that took no ground and learned no terrain carried no information. A crusade that adopts nothing is a legal outcome.

Counsel never touches the tree; implementation history is the contamination that makes a judge worse. Builders are cheap and disposable; the judgment is not.

## The form

Fill this with the user before wave 0. Everything outside it is loop; everything inside is mission.

```
MISSION   what gets better, and the thing you may never do to get it
NUMBER    the exact command and which number; the secondary numbers read beside it
EYE       what the eye sees, how often, and whether it outranks the number
GATES     what must stay green; the honesty bar no candidate lowers
WIDTH     builders per wave, minutes per builder
DRY       the rule that ends the crusade; polish is a stop, not a wave
ABSENT    verdicts the commander may make while the user is away; each re-openable
LEDGER    where verdicts persist
```

## Wave

Baseline twice, byte-identical, or the first hypothesis is "make it deterministic". Then: send builders on distinct seams (reject a slate where two share one); harvest reports; give a verdict per builder, ADOPT / KILL / FALSIFIED, with its stake; graft; reshape. Two dry waves end the crusade. Falsified mechanisms persist as Tried & rejected. At a plateau, change the approach before you send the same wave again.

Mechanics (sessions, worktrees, waiters) are `execute`'s. Builder and eye briefs are `delegate`'s.
