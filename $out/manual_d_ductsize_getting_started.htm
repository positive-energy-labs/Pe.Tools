Manual D Ductsize Getting Started

Watch Video Tutorials at elitesoft.com

Here is the typical sequence of things you should do when using graphic Manual D Ductsize.

1.After you have drawn your floor plan on the Drawing Board window for the load calculation, change to the "Manual D Ductsize Drawing Objects for Rhvac 10" catalog in the Catalog Browser.

2.Drag and drop a Supply Main Trunk object onto the drawing.

3.Move the Supply Main Trunk object into place by clicking and dragging the yellow grab handles at its endpoints.

4.Right click the Supply Main Trunk object and from the popup menu click "General Duct Settings | Duct Defaults for this System."

5.On the Duct Defaults for System 1 dialog, enter the default properties you want to use for trunks and runouts in this system, such as the duct material and the desired static pressure drop per 100 ft.  Click OK when you are done.

6.Right click the Supply Main Trunk object again and from the popup menu click "Auto Create/Refresh Runouts."

7.On the Auto Create/Refresh Runouts dialog, select all the rooms in System 1 and click OK to create the runouts.

8.Draw any additional trunks you need and connect all of the runouts and trunks together.

9.Click the menu "File | Select Reports," then on the Select Reports dialog, check the boxes for the Manual D Ductsize reports and click the Preview Button.
