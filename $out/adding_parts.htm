Adding Parts to Your Project Bill of Materials

Bill of Materials Topics

In addition to the ductwork parts that are added automatically, you can also add non-takeoff parts (ductwork or otherwise) to your project bill of materials.  There are three ways you can add parts:

1.  Copy a part from your database:  In the Project Bill of Materials window's toolbar, click "Part | Add Part from Database."  Then on the Bill of Materials Parts Database window find the part you want to add.  You may need to redefine your query first to help you find the part you want.  Once you find the part you want select it and then click the "Add Selected Item to Project Bill of Materials" button.

2.  Create a part that is not in your database:  In the Project Bill of Materials window's toolbar, click "Part | Add Non-Database Part to Section" (as shown below), then select which section to add your new part to.  After the part is added you can edit the description, part number, cost, etc..

3.  Copy the HVAC equipment that you selected for the current project:  To add the air conditioner, furnace, etc. that you selected for the current project, in the Project Bill of Materials window's toolbar click "Part | Add HVAC Equipment from Current Project."
