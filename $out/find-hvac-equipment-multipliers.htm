Find HVAC Equipment Multipliers

To open this dialog click the Multipliers button on the Find HVAC Equipment window.

Cooling Minimum, Cooling Maximum, etc.:  Specifies the percentage by which to multiply the heating or cooling load in order to set the capacity ranges on the Find HVAC Equipment window when opened from the System Data window.

For example, if a system has a cooling load of 22,000 Btuh and you want to see standard air conditioning units that range from 100% to 120% of that load, enter 100 and 120 in the Cooling Minimum and Cooling Maximum inputs.  Then when you click the Find HVAC Equipment window on the System Data window's toolbar, the system data window will open with 22 and 26 entered for the Cool Cap - Min and Cool Cap - Max inputs.

Heat Pump Inputs:  Separate inputs are provided for heat pumps in case you want to enter different criteria, since the process of selecting a heat pump often involves different considerations than for non-heat pump equipment.

OK Button:  Saves your changes and closes this dialog.

Cancel Button:  Closes this dialog without making any changes.
