Room (Rhvac Drawing Objects and Chvac Drawing Objects)

The Room figure is a polygon that has 4 or more Wall figures around its perimeter that define the shape of the room.  It also has Special Properties that represent all the behind the scenes information about the room, such as system number and number of people.  You can resize the Room figure by either clicking on the room background polygon and then dragging one of its white grab handles, or by moving its Wall figures individually.  To move a room, click inside the room to select it and then drag it with the mouse.  Be careful not to position rooms where they overlap each other.

Special Commands

Room Name Options:  Displays the Room Name Options dialog, where one or more of the drawing's room names can be appended with the room area, system number and zone number.

Zone Colors:  Displays the Zone Colors dialog, where the colors for all the different zones in the project can be set to any color.

Delete Room:  Removes this room from the drawing and the Rhvac project.

To Create Non Rectangular Rooms, Bay Windows, etc.

1.Ctrl+double click on a Wall figure to divide it into two Wall figures.

2.Click on one of the Walls and drag its endpoint to a new location.

3.Ctrl+double click again to divide another wall into two sections.

4.Drag the new wall's endpoint to the new location to complete the shape of the room.
