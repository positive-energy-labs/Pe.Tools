Adding an Adjoining Room (Tutorial Step 5)

                               

1.Drag another "Room/Walls" object from the Catalog Browser and drop it just to the right of the existing room on your drawing.

2.Click on the colored background polygon of the new room and drag it so that it just adjoins the existing room, as pictured below:

3.The wall that the two rooms have in common is an interior wall and will have no heating or cooling load, so we need to tell Rhvac not to include the wall in the load calculations.  Double click on the wall to set its Included property to False.  When the wall is not included, it becomes transparent and has a dotted line type.  The drawing should now look like this:
