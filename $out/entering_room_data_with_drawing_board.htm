Entering Room Data With Drawing Board (Tutorial Step 7)

                               

Now let's draw the rooms that are in this project on the Drawing Board window.  We will be making a floor plan that is similar to the one in the Sample project, which was included when you first installed Rhvac.  For another tutorial on entering room data with Drawing Board, click the menu "Help | Drawing Board Tutorial."

1.Click the Project menu and click Room Data Entry Mode.  The Room Data Entry Mode dialog opens

2.Select "Enter room data with Drawing Board" if it isn't already selected and click OK.

3.Click the Project menu and click Drawing Board.  The New Drawing dialog appears.

4.On the Templates tab of the New Drawing dialog, double click the icon for the template named "8.5 x 11 Eighth Scale with Border.dtp."  The Drawing Board window opens.

5.On the Catalog Browser of the Drawing Board window, if the catalog "Rhvac Drawing Objects" is not open click the Open Catalog toolbar button on the Catalog Browser's toolbar.  The Open Catalog File dialog opens.

6.On the Open Catalog File dialog, select "Rhvac Drawing Objects" and click Open.

7.In the Catalog Browser, click and drag the icon "Rooms/Walls" and drop it on the drawing window.

8.Size the new room to be 10 ft. wide by 12 ft. long.

9.Right click on the colored background of the new room and click "Special..." from the popup menu.

10.On the Object Properties dialog, click the down arrow button beside the Name property and select "Kitchen" from the dropdown list of common room names.

11.Double click on the wall on the right hand side of this room to make it so it is not included.  The outline of the wall turns to a dashed line.  Do the same thing for the wall at the bottom of the room.

12.Click and drag the "Window" icon from the Catalog Browser and drop it on the wall at the top of the room.  The window appears and is positioned in the wall.

13.Click and drag the "Room/Walls" icon again and drop it on the drawing window.

14.Size this new room to be 14 ft. wide by 15 ft. long.

15.Position this new room near the Kitchen, but for now leave it a little bit to the right of it.

16.Double click the wall on the wall on the left-hand side of this new room to make it so it will not be included in the calculation.

17.Using the same procedure described above for the Kitchen, name this new room "Living Room".

18.Position this new room so it is adjacent and to the right of the Kitchen.  Position the room so that the walls at the top of these two rooms are even with each other.

19.Drag and drop a new room and name it "Bedroom".

20.Right click about 1/3 of the way from the left on the horizontal wall at the top of this new room and click "Divide Wall Here" from the popup menu.  The wall divides into two walls.

21.Of the two horizontal walls created in the above step, double click the one on the right-hand side.

22.Repeat the previous procedure for the vertical wall on the right-hand side, and double click on the wall closer to the top of the room to make it so it is not included.

23.Position the Bedroom below and to the left of the Kitchen so that its top is adjacent to the Kitchen and its right hand wall is adjacent to the Living Room.

24.Drag and drop a few windows and doors around the perimeter of the building.

25.Drag a North Arrow figure from the Catalog Browser and drop it on the drawing, positioning it near the rooms you've just created.

26.Right click on the North Arrow figure and click "SW" from the popup menu.  This changes the direction of the plan so that north is now facing toward the bottom left corner of the drawing.

27.Close the Drawing Board window.  This saves the drawing to the temporary file.  

28.Click the Save button in the toolbar.  The project file and the drawing file are both saved to your Projects folder.
