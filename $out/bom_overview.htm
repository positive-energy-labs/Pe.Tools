Bill of Materials Overview

Bill of Materials Topics

Bill of Materials is a separately licensed component that works within Rhvac that creates a highly customizable list of parts for any HVAC job.  For projects that include ducts drawn with Manual D Ductsize, it will also do a complete takeoff of your duct systems and generate parts for each unique duct, fitting, grille and register.

The list of parts chosen for a particular project is called the project bill of materials.  The window on which the parts are shown is called the Project Bill of Materials window.

A database of thousands of ductwork and other parts is included.  You can browse and manage the database on the Bill of Materials Parts Database window.  You can add your own parts, either one at a time or by importing multiple parts from your own spreadsheet or database file.  You can add parts to your project bill of materials by copying them from the database, or by adding them directly to your project bill of materials.

Each part can be edited in place in the project bill of materials.  You simply click on the cell you want to edit and type in a new value.

You can customize the layout of the project bill of materials to suit your needs.  You can choose from over 70 columns of available data, such as sale price, dealer cost, quantity override, part number, tax rate, etc..  You can save layouts for later use in other projects.  You can have one layout for the Project Bill of Materials window and another layout for the printed report.
