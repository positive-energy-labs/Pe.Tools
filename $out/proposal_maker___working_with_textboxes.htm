Working with Text Frames

Proposal Maker Main Topics (List)                Proposal Maker Menus (List)

Adding a text frame to a document gives you the ability to have a section of text that can be moved and resized independently from the rest of the document.  A text frame on the document can either be in the mode "move with text" or "fixed position on page."  A "fixed position on page" text frame can be moved anywhere on the document (by dragging) and will remain at the location you move it to even as you change text on the rest of the document.  One in the "move with text" mode can be resized freely, but you can only move it to places on the document where it is already possible for text to be.  To add text to a text frame, click inside it and type your text.

Moving Text Frames

To move a text frame, click on the frame away from any of its eight grab handles and drag it with the mouse.  If it is in "fixed position on page" mode you can move it freely anywhere on the document page.  If it is in the "move with text" mode you can only move it to places on the page where it is already possible for text to be.

Sizing Text Frames

All text frames are freely sizable, regardless of mode.  To resize a text frame, click and drag one of its eight grab handles.

Miscellaneous

You can add a native picture to a text frame by clicking inside the text frame and clicking "Insert | Picture."

You cannot insert a picture field inside a text frame.
