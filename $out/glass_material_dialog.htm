Glass Material Dialog

Navigation:  Program Windows > Project Windows > Room Data > Inputs > Glass >

Glass Material Dialog

Click the button beside the Glass Material input on the Room Data or Default Room Data window to open this dialog.

Favorites Tab:  Lists the glass you have added to your Favorites collection.  Double click an item in the list to select that glass.

Recent Tab:  Lists your most recently selected glass materials.  Double click an item in the list to select that glass.

Custom Tab:  Lists the custom glass items that you have created by clicking the Create Custom button.  Double click an item in the list to select that glass item.

Main Category Tabs:  Determines the type of glass categories listed in the category list (the one in the middle of this window).  Different glass categories have different heating and cooling load characteristics.

Sub Category List:  The middle list on this dialog.  Displays the sub categories available under the current main category tab.  Click on an item in the list and the bottom list will show the various glass items available in that sub category.

Glass Item List:  The bottom list on this dialog.  Displays the glass available in the current sub category.  Double click on a glass in this list to select it.  Click any of the column headers to sort the list by that column.  Click the same column again to reverse the sort order.

Note: The terms "with break" and "no break" in the Description column refer to whether or not the metal on the interior of the frame of the window connects directly with the metal on the exterior of the frame.  "With break" means that there is a gap or insulating material between the inside metal and outside metal, which makes for a lower u-value for the window.

Create Custom Button:  Opens the Custom Glass dialog, which lets you enter a custom glass material to be added to your list of Custom glass.  Custom glass are always the bottom item in any of the sub category lists.

Organize Button:  Displays a menu that lets you select to organize your Favorites, Recent or Custom glass.

Add To Favorites Button:  Adds the currently selected glass to your Favorites list.

OK Button:  Copies the currently selected glass back to the Room Data or other window and closes this dialog.

Cancel Button:  Closes this dialog without copying a glass to the Room Data or other window.

Glass Options

The selections in the Glass Options area are for the current glass only (such as on the Room Data window).  These properties are recalled whenever you select a Favorite or a Recent glass.

Reset Button:  Resets all of the Glass Options inputs back to their default values.

General

Geometry Type (Type):  Selecting a geometry other than flat will increase the heating and cooling load for the glass.  The heating and cooling HTM values for a bay window will be 1.15 times that of a flat window.  The HTM of a garden window will be 2.75 times that of a flat window for heating, and 2.00 times that of a flat window for cooling.

Foreground Reflectance (Ground Refl):  The cooling HTM of a window is affected by the reflectivity of the foreground surfaces because the solar gain depends on the amount of direct and diffuse radiation striking the glass.  The default value for this input is 0.20.

Skylight Tilt:  This input is only available if the selected glass is a skylight.  The tilt angle will affect the amount of solar gain for a skylight.  If the skylight is horizontal, enter zero.

Internal Shading

Type:  Select the internal shading option from the list.  Light colored shading devices generally reduce the heat gain of a window more than do dark colored ones.

Amt. Drawn:  Enter the amount that the internal shading is drawn, in percent.  If the shading device is fully closed, enter 100.

Insect Screen

Type:  An insect screen will decrease the amount of solar gain for a window.  The correction factor used for an inside screen is 0.90, while that of an outside screen is 0.80.

Coverage:  Enter the amount of the window that the insect screen covers, in percent.

External Shade Screen

Coefficient:  If there is an external shade screen, enter the coefficient for the screen.  If there is no screen, enter 1 in this input.

Coverage:  Enter the amount of the window that the external shade screen covers, in percent.
