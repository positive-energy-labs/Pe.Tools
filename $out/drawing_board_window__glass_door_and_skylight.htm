Window, Glass Door and Skylight (Rhvac Drawing Objects, Chvac Drawing Objects)

The window and glass door figures are LineShape figures, which means you can drag either of the endpoints and the figure will stretch as you drag it.  Once a window or glass door is placed in a wall it cannot be moved to another wall. If moved outside of its wall, the figure will reposition itself back into the wall.

The Skylight figure can be resized to any dimension as long as its boundaries do not exceed the boundaries of the rectangle containing the Room object.  When you move a room, the Skylight figure will move along with it.

These figures have several Special Properties, which you can enter on the Special tab on the Object Properties window.

The middle yellow handle on the Window and Glass door object lets you drag the figure in a parallel manner along the length of its parent wall.

Showing Glass Dimensions:  For window and glass door objects you can set the "Show Glass Dimensions" property to True and the object will include a user-movable text object that shows the window's width and height.

Special Commands

Window, Glass Door:  Sets the appearance of the glass figure to either a single window or a sliding glass door.

Copy Glass:  Copies the selected window or glass door to the clipboard, so you can later paste it into another glass or into a Wall object.

Paste Glass:  Pastes the properties of the glass that is in the clipboard into the current glass.  Only available if you have previously copied a window or glass door by clicking the Copy Glass command.  This command is also available in the Wall object's popup menu.

Delete:  Remove the window or glass door from the drawing.

To create bay windows see the help on the Room or Zone figure.
