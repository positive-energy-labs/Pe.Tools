Deleting Parts from Your Project Bill of Materials

Bill of Materials Topics

See also: Excluding Parts, Part Groups

Here are the types of parts that can exist in your project bill of materials, and the options on how you can delete them.

1.  Ductwork parts from the automatic takeoff:  You can set the Drawing Board properties "Include Duct in BOM," "Include Fittings in BOM," and "Include Register in BOM" (shown below) to False to prevent parts for a particular duct from being included in the project bill of materials.

Another way to remove a part from the project bill of materials is to set the Exclude property to False on the project bill of materials.  One way to do that is to right click on the part and click "Exclude this Part" from the popup menu, as shown below.

Another way is to use the Part Groups feature, and to assign the part to a part group that is currently being hidden.

2.  Parts not from the automatic takeoff that you have added:  For these kinds of parts, simply right click on a part and click "Part | Delete Current Item" from the popup menu, as shown below.
