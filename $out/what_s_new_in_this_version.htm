What's New In Version 10

Here are some of the new features included in version 10 of Rhvac.

·ACCA Form 310 data saved in project and printed on form:  Saves you from having to fill out the form by hand if your local authorities are requiring you to provide this form.

·New inputs for SEER2, EER2, and HSPF2 efficiency ratings:  Lets you enter these new, more accurate efficiency ratings on the Equipment tab of the System Data window.  These values can show up on the following reports:  Room Load Summary, Equipment Data, and Quick Sales Proposal.

·Negative overhang offsets allowed:  You can enter a negative number for an overhang offset, for those cases where the overhang slopes down so that the bottom of it is below the top of the window.

·Export to EnergyPro version 9:  Added an option to export to the latest version of the EnergyPro program, version 9.  The list of versions now includes 5 through 9.

·Metal wall category:  Added a metal wall category to frame walls on the Wall Material window, along with 4 metal wall materials.

·Show trunk airflows on the drawing:  Added a "Show Flow" property for Manual D Ductsize trunks on the Drawing Board window.

·Improved dropdown help list appearance:  For some inputs, modified some dropdown help lists in order to show all of the items without scrolling.
