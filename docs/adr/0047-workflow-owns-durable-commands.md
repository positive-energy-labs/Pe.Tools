# 0047: Workflow owns durable commands

Date: 2026-11-16. Status: deferred, not adopted (amended 2026-11-17; see Consequences). Source: accepted [Converge map](../features/host/CONVERGE-MAP.md). Amends [ADR 0029](0029-effect-is-the-standard-library-one-fiber-per-request.md) rules 3, 9, 10, 14 and 15; [ADR 0031](0031-recording-at-host-input-services-history-in-the-shell.md) rules 1 and 10; [ADR 0033](0033-one-host-read-graph-history-is-host-publication.md)'s ActionJournal survival clause; and [ADR 0039](0039-two-failure-policies-by-class.md)'s journal discard policy. Other rules stand.

## Context

The rejection of workflow and cluster assumed a second owner beside ActionJournal. Converge replaces that owner. Discovery found that workflow joins changed payloads under one key, reruns an in-flight Activity after a crash, and does not make interrupt a durable Stop during an Activity. An engine primitive does not transfer the journal's domain responsibilities automatically.

## Decision

1. `CommandWorkflow` owns durable commands through Effect `workflow`, `ClusterWorkflowEngine`, `SingleRunner` and Effect `sql` over SQLite in a `stateTree` Engine row. This supersedes ADR 0029 rule 9 only for workflow/cluster/sql as the replacement owner. It does not adopt eventlog, KeyValueStore or TxRef as parallel state owners. Keep the exact Effect pin; unstable modules are allowed, and a bump is a separate change.
2. Admission writes a create-only binding of attempt id to intent digest, validated caller capability and exact target before execution or acceptance. Identical resubmissions join, changed bindings refuse. The workflow key is the attempt id only, never id plus digest. Admission owns authorize/preflight, FIFO admission and claims; claim release requires dispatch evidence. Recheck live-lane authority, revocation and target freshness at continuation and dispatch; an earlier authorization Activity cannot authorize a write on a recording. Activities own plan, native steps, host effects and consumed Work retirement under [ADR 0046](0046-effect-identity-belongs-to-the-effect-owner.md).
3. Causal ownership crosses processes as validated data: owner capability, parent id, cancellation policy and terminal state. Durable submission precedes acknowledgment. Stop records a durable signal, requests native `op.cancel`, and prevents later steps. Observer loss never stops a command. Parent Stop addresses its policy-defined descendants; parent settlement waits for required descendant reads and restoration evidence. Completion-persist failure settles local waiters with honest uncertainty and cannot publish success or release a native slot on a timer.
4. Preserve the serial native slot per process incarnation, its bounded FIFO, connection-epoch fence and evidence-gated release. Cancel bypasses its queue. Shared reads and owner scopes remain separate from command lifetime; RcMap is not evidence that a retiring native dispatch has ended.
5. OD1: retire consumed unchanged Work on completed outcome, never a newer revision. Re-stage on committed-in-ended-incarnation-unsaved after a controlled end. After a crash, offer re-stage to the person because recovery copies may exist. Keep retired intent recoverable in command history. Save As, sync, failed save, undo and later deletion remain gaps, not inferred save knowledge. ADR 0038 held acts remain unchanged.
6. One writer holds each engine database, including competing normal and no-Revit hosts. Active projections retire only after dependents settle. Indexed durable authority serves cursor queries, not full-history residency. Configure finite entry/byte limits and overload results for queues, caches and subscribers, plus finite history retention and shutdown bounds. Retain compact identity/outcome and referenced causal joins beyond telemetry pruning; expiry must never reopen an old attempt. If authority must be removed, fence its admission generation as in rule 8. S4 must state and measure concrete bounds before landing.
7. Journal and receipt producers remain swappable recorded input services under ADR 0031. Replays answer recorded answered/refused/unknown outcomes and never fetch a live fallback. Rebuild receipt and History publication from committed durable authority after a crash, not a subscriber. History remains what the host published under ADR 0033, with no new write or retry authority.
8. The engine database takes ADR 0039's derived journal class: incompatible/corrupt state is discarded with one visible notice while preserving prior bytes aside. Before a replacement accepts work, persist a discard epoch outside that database (OD9). Refuse old or unprovable admission generations, including pre-epoch resends, rather than recreating effect authority. Missing, corrupt or unwritable fence means command admission stays closed; unrelated host reads may start. No legacy migration or replay from discarded state. Authored keep and foreign policies remain intact.

## Consequences

Amendment, 2026-11-17, root default on the seam: Workflow is deferred, not adopted. ActionJournal stays the one durable owner of commands, and native truth comes from the SDK's recorded attempt response. The Family Foundry crossing showed that the journal already owns admission with changed-intent refusal, plan claims, receipts, Stop and crash settlement behind every door, and that the Workflow core had no production importer, so it was deleted instead of connected (`ts/apps/host/src/commands/`, last present at `73d17472`). The decision above is the record of a design that was not taken up. A later S4 is a separate owner decision, judged by the journal's measured defects, and the owner can reopen this default.

FND-02 is an unresolved S4 gate: "attempt id only" names the logical admission key, not Effect's tag-dependent execution hash. Admission must freeze the original Workflow tag/execution id, recipe/code compatibility identity and derived step request identities. Resume must use a compatible frozen continuation or refuse before any new effect; current handler registration and Activity name/attempt reuse do not prove code meaning. UNPROVEN[upgrade falsifier not run]: complete A, suspend before B, change A's meaning or insert a step, and restart; also resend through a renamed Workflow tag. Require no mismatched result reuse or second execution/native identity. Preserve responsibility-transfer D2 before deleting the old validator.

FND-03 is an unresolved S4/S6 gate: the external discard epoch must classify the original admission, not a retry's sender timestamp, resend flag or naked UUID. Retain trustworthy original epoch provenance or enough safety identity outside disposable history; retries cannot refresh it. OD8/OD9 remain the accepted defaults, but an epoch alone is insufficient proof. UNPROVEN[discard/retention falsifier not run]: act after the started mark, lose the result, discard the DB, advance the epoch and restart twice. Old or unprovably new requests, including absent resend metadata and advanced sender clocks, must cause zero additional effects; an explicitly new authored attempt must still work. Repeat across retention and crashes between discard steps. Root `.artifacts/runs/converge-20261116-wave0/foundation-smeller.md` holds both findings and their pinned source lineage.

Delete ActionJournal only after every responsibility-transfer row has an owner and a failure-boundary falsifier. OD8 supplies the conservative owner for E6-E8; it is not evidence of delivery. Retain old machinery until its replacement proves the behavior, then remove it in S6.

S4 must falsify changed-intent admission, descendant Stop/settlement, completion-persist failure, corrupt DB and second-writer admission, restart/resend after discard, bounded retention/shutdown, and handler upgrade between crash and resume. Packed-host SQLite evidence does not prove installer behavior. All replacement runtime claims are UNPROVEN in S0.
