# 0050: Workflow owns durable commands

Date: 2026-11-16. Status: rejected by the owner, 2026-11-17 (see Consequences). The journal stays the one durable owner of commands. Source: the earlier [Converge map](../features/host/CONVERGE-MAP.md), in Git at `d00b801f`. It proposed to amend [ADR 0029](0029-effect-is-the-standard-library-one-fiber-per-request.md) rules 3, 9, 10, 14 and 15; [ADR 0031](0031-recording-at-host-input-services-history-in-the-shell.md) rules 1 and 10; [ADR 0033](0033-one-host-read-graph-history-is-host-publication.md)'s ActionJournal survival clause; and [ADR 0039](0039-two-failure-policies-by-class.md)'s journal discard policy. None of those amendments apply.

## Context

The rejection of workflow and cluster assumed a second owner beside ActionJournal. Converge replaces that owner. Discovery found that workflow joins changed payloads under one key, reruns an in-flight Activity after a crash, and does not make interrupt a durable Stop during an Activity. An engine primitive does not transfer the journal's domain responsibilities automatically.

## Decision

1. `CommandWorkflow` owns durable commands through Effect `workflow`, `ClusterWorkflowEngine`, `SingleRunner` and Effect `sql` over SQLite in a `stateTree` Engine row. This supersedes ADR 0029 rule 9 only for workflow/cluster/sql as the replacement owner. It does not adopt eventlog, KeyValueStore or TxRef as parallel state owners. Keep the exact Effect pin; unstable modules are allowed, and a bump is a separate change.
2. Admission writes a create-only binding of attempt id to intent digest, validated caller capability and exact target before execution or acceptance. Identical resubmissions join, changed bindings refuse. The workflow key is the attempt id only, never id plus digest. Admission owns authorize/preflight, FIFO admission and claims; claim release requires dispatch evidence. Recheck live-lane authority, revocation and target freshness at continuation and dispatch; an earlier authorization Activity cannot authorize a write on a recording. Activities own plan, native steps, host effects and consumed Work retirement under [ADR 0049](0049-effect-identity-belongs-to-the-effect-owner.md).
3. Causal ownership crosses processes as validated data: owner capability, parent id, cancellation policy and terminal state. Durable submission precedes acknowledgment. Stop records a durable signal, requests native `op.cancel`, and prevents later steps. Observer loss never stops a command. Parent Stop addresses its policy-defined descendants; parent settlement waits for required descendant reads and restoration evidence. Completion-persist failure settles local waiters with honest uncertainty and cannot publish success or release a native slot on a timer.
4. Preserve the serial native slot per process incarnation, its bounded FIFO, connection-epoch fence and evidence-gated release. Cancel bypasses its queue. Shared reads and owner scopes remain separate from command lifetime; RcMap is not evidence that a retiring native dispatch has ended.
5. OD1: retire consumed unchanged Work on completed outcome, never a newer revision. Re-stage on committed-in-ended-incarnation-unsaved after a controlled end. After a crash, offer re-stage to the person because recovery copies may exist. Keep retired intent recoverable in command history. Save As, sync, failed save, undo and later deletion remain gaps, not inferred save knowledge. ADR 0038 held acts remain unchanged.
6. One writer holds each engine database, including competing normal and no-Revit hosts. Active projections retire only after dependents settle. Indexed durable authority serves cursor queries, not full-history residency. Configure finite entry/byte limits and overload results for queues, caches and subscribers, plus finite history retention and shutdown bounds. Retain compact identity/outcome and referenced causal joins beyond telemetry pruning; expiry must never reopen an old attempt. If authority must be removed, fence its admission generation as in rule 8. S4 must state and measure concrete bounds before landing.
7. Journal and receipt producers remain swappable recorded input services under ADR 0031. Replays answer recorded answered/refused/unknown outcomes and never fetch a live fallback. Rebuild receipt and History publication from committed durable authority after a crash, not a subscriber. History remains what the host published under ADR 0033, with no new write or retry authority.
8. The engine database takes ADR 0039's derived journal class: incompatible/corrupt state is discarded with one visible notice while preserving prior bytes aside. Before a replacement accepts work, persist a discard epoch outside that database (OD9). Refuse old or unprovable admission generations, including pre-epoch resends, rather than recreating effect authority. Missing, corrupt or unwritable fence means command admission stays closed; unrelated host reads may start. No legacy migration or replay from discarded state. Authored keep and foreign policies remain intact.

## Consequences

Rejected, 2026-11-17, after the Converge spike (`.artifacts/runs/converge-20261117-bspike/COMPARISON.md` in the root checkout). The Workflow column (B) gave a false answer on case 3f-post: the write landed, B resent the step at boot before the bridge reconnected, settled it as `refused … nothing ran`, and Undo rolled the Work back past the real write. PROVEN[session+browser, acc-bspike-b2, c48135d3]. That was B's third crash-path defect, and all three sat in its adapter, because the engine's redelivery does not own dispatch certainty. The trimmed journal (A′) was the only column with no false answer in either round.

Owner: "no, youve convinced me B is not worth. principle needs to meet pragmatism, the current journal was hardened over hundreds of agent writer hours and if effect doesnt make the hard part easy then is just an aesthetic choice."

So ActionJournal stays the one durable owner, and native truth comes from the SDK's recorded attempt response. The inactive core (`ts/apps/host/src/commands/`, last present at `73d17472`) was deleted, not connected. The Decision above is the record of the rejected design: none of its rules bind, and its S4 gates (FND-02 upgrade identity, FND-03 discard provenance) lapse with it. The amendments it made to ADRs 0029, 0031, 0033 and 0039 do not apply. A later proposal to move durable commands off the journal needs a named field failure or a driven false answer ([Converge map](../features/host/CONVERGE-MAP.md), kernel rule 1).
