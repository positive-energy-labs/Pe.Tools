## Tried & rejected

- 2026-09-01, implicit working-copy creation in the fallback stranded a documentless session and blocked cloud models; normal open behavior is the settled replacement.
- 2026-09-06, exact wall-line polygonization under-partitions when lines stop short; full DWG layer inclusion adds millwork as walls, and stud-aware closing worsened every control. Enclosure authority cannot be replaced by more geometry indiscriminately.
- 2026-09-06, per-face rectification loses shared boundaries; fit each shared boundary once. A fixed relative-area gate also rejects legitimate growth disproportionately in small rooms.
- 2026-09-06, rounded fixture coordinates hid an exact-coordinate shared-node defect that simplification widened into 0.005496 sqft overlap. Keep exact captures and emitted coverage checks; neither tolerance relaxation nor rounding is a repair.
- 2026-09-07, treating generic IFC proxy geometry as occupancy floor/overhead is not justified by category alone. Honest `no-floor` holds must remain visible until surface provenance is established; count gains do not establish room accuracy.
