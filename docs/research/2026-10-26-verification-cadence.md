# Verification at substantial checkpoints

Broad verification and durable test consolidation now belong at substantial checkpoints. During fast exploration, the agent should expose the changed experience, check the behavior that informs the next decision, and preserve useful evidence. This is the user's explicit reversal of an older operating rule. The evidence supports removing that rule; it does not establish an OpenAI-specific tendency or a universal optimum for test frequency.

The user recalled requesting the earlier full-gate posture and now said to “totally unwind this posturing.” Their examples are before merging a worktree, before publishing a release, after a large accumulation of messy code-is-the-spec changes, and after roughly one to three major campaign waves. Scale and coupling determine the checkpoint; the examples are not a rigid counter. Both costs matter equally: tests can freeze an unsettled implementation, and passing tests can miss the experience the user needs to assess.

## Applied guidance

- [index](../../.agents/skills/root.index/SKILL.md) no longer requires one test per rule or assumes visible numbers cannot need focused invariant coverage. Progress is behavior with an owner and evidence appropriate to the claim.
- [prove](../../.agents/skills/lens.prove/SKILL.md) replaces mandatory repetition of every reported proof and a regression test for every discovered bug. It retains immediate focused checks for consequential uncertainty, data loss, unsafe mutation and contracts. Completeness must be compared with requested behavior and actual consumers, not certified by the implementation's own inventory.
- [execute](../../.agents/skills/slot.execute/SKILL.md), [TypeScript guidance](../../ts/AGENTS.md) and [web guidance](../../ts/apps/web/AGENTS.md) replace the full-verifier-before-every-done-claim rule with substantial checkpoints. During iteration, manual interaction, bounded pe-browser/Vitest flows and relevant focused checks are valid evidence. After failure, rerun affected stages and widen when changed dependencies or unresolved failures require it.
- [ADR 0023](../adr/0023-verification-at-substantial-checkpoints.md) supersedes the cadence and exclusive-proof clauses of ADR 0012. Its command, CI, lane setup and generated-contract checks remain. The old ADR's status and the host/skill ledgers point to the reversal.

The separate [delegation changes](2026-10-26-conversational-delegation.md) are also applied: delegate most sustained independent work and keep the main agent available for discussion and joint inspection. No new stance is needed. No product source, test inventory or CI command was changed by this work. The guidance, decision records and durable reports are recorded together in one commit. Raw research and trial artifacts remain in the local gitignored run directories.
